Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about working together. If yours isn't here, let's talk.

How long does a typical project take?
Every project is different. Discovery and prototyping usually take 1-2 weeks, then we'll outline a timeline in the proposal based on scope.
Do you work with clients outside your area?
Absolutely. Most client communication happens over video calls and email. Location doesn't matter.
What if I'm not sure exactly what I need?
That's what discovery is for. We'll talk through your challenges and I'll help clarify what solution makes sense.
How do you handle changes during the project?
Small adjustments are normal. Larger scope changes are discussed together and may adjust the timeline or investment.
What happens after handover?
You get documentation and training. I'm available for questions and can provide ongoing support if needed.

Still have questions?

Tell me about your project — I usually respond within 48 hours.
