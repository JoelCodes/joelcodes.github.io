Multi-Channel Inventory Sync

Automation

A growing e-commerce retailer was selling on Amazon, Shopify, and eBay simultaneously. Inventory counts were updated manually across platforms, leading to overselling, angry customers, and hours of daily reconciliation work.

The Challenge

A growing e-commerce retailer was selling on Amazon, Shopify, and eBay simultaneously. Inventory counts were updated manually across platforms, leading to overselling, angry customers, and hours of daily reconciliation work.

The Solution

We built an automation that syncs inventory levels in real-time across all sales channels. When a sale happens on any platform, stock counts update everywhere within seconds. The system also alerts the team when items run low and generates reorder reports automatically.
