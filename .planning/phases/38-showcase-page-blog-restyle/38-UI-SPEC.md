### Showcase dark recipe (D-04 — derived from landing `117:103`, flag as derived at the gate)

No dark Showcase frame exists. Apply the landing section-level dark treatments verbatim
(values verified from `src/pages/index.astro`, Phase 37 gate-approved):

| Section background | Light | Dark |
|---|---|---|
| Sea-glass gradient | `linear-gradient(to_bottom,#E6F1F1,#D2E7E7)` | `linear-gradient(to_bottom,#123640,#0C2228)` |
| Sea-glass subtle gradient | `linear-gradient(to_bottom,#EFF7F6,#E6F1F1)` | `linear-gradient(to_bottom,#10303A,#0E2B33)` |

**Rule 1 (landing CR-01):** gradient backgrounds go in `class` (arbitrary-value syntax), NOT inline `style`,
so `dark:` can override: `class="[background:linear-gradient(...)] dark:[background:linear-gradient(...)]"`.
Which showcase sections take which pair = FIDELITY-GAP from `12:3` light extraction; dark pair follows the
light pair's landing mapping.

**Blog dark treatment:** follows the same D-04/landing-recipe precedent whether frames are drafted
light-only or both (discretion call below). Token-based colors flip automatically; any gradient literal in
the approved frames maps to its landing dark pair.

     useDarkModeMediaQuery: false,
   })
