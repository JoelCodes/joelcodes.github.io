  target is the site root `/`, not an open redirect to any external/attacker-controlled URL. No
  user-supplied redirect target exists.

  is the inverse of exposure — a leak would be blog content unintentionally REMAINING indexed. Both

  `/blog` in sitemap and no blog HTML in dist.
No ASVS L1 auth/session/crypto items apply (no auth surface in chrome/IA).
